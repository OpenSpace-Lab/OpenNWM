## Scientific limits

The opening film is an editorial composition of first-person source views, not a
model output. Generated human and robot overlays have been removed. Moon footage is LuSNAR simulation; the Mars
segment animates a single real Zhurong image. All these distinctions are visible
in the page and recorded in the media provenance.
Within each journey segment, decoded source frames remain unblended to avoid
double-image artifacts; neighboring scenes use a 0.18-second cross-dissolve.

The current Nav15 Stage 2 joint100k checkpoint fails to preserve the planetary
environment in the tested Moon, Mars and LuSNAR predictions. Outputs drift into
terrestrial buildings/interiors. The website labels these as stress-test failures.
Fixed-initial-context diagnostic runs also failed; they are retained separately
and do not replace the consistent autoregressive demo protocol.

Custom-path outputs are 16 predicted RGB frames at 224 × 224 and 4 FPS. The
four-second playback convention does not establish physical mission timing.
Paths are normalized planar controls, not metric, collision-free navigation plans.
The demo uses VAE posterior means, unlike benchmark posterior sampling. These
qualitative results are not new benchmark scores.
